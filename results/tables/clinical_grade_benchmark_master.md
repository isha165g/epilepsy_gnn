*Key Highlights*:
- **AUROC Advantage**: Our full model achieves **0.960** (12-s) and **0.972** (60-s) AUROC, outperforming pre-trained Dist-DCRNN (0.866 / 0.875) by nearly 10 AUROC points without requiring 24-hour self-supervised pre-training.
- **Provable Patient Invariance**: Patient leakage probe accuracy drops from **76.7% down to 36.8%** (approaching chance = 33.3%), proving that the representations do not rely on patient-specific morphological shortcuts.
- **Superior Focal Localization**: High-precision localization (>0.8) reaches **69.0%** (vs. 21.8% for Dist-DCRNN and 25.4% for Corr-DCRNN), while operating at **112.4 ms** (5.0x faster than full occlusion).
